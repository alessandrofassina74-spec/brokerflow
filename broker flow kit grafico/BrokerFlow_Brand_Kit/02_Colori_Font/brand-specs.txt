BROKERFLOW — BRAND SPECIFICATIONS

Brand name: BrokerFlow
Tagline: IL TUO MUTUO, SEMPLICE

CORE COLORS
Navy: #071B3A — primary text, dark backgrounds, trust
Blue: #006BFF — technology, action, Flow accent
Cyan: #12CFEA — energy, flow, digital accent
Aqua: #00D4B8 — optional gradient endpoint
White: #FFFFFF — primary background
Light: #F4F7FB — secondary background

PRIMARY GRADIENT
BrokerFlow Flow Gradient: #006BFF → #12CFEA → #00D4B8
Direction: left to right / upward where appropriate.

WORDMARK
Broker: Navy #071B3A
Flow: Blue-to-Cyan gradient
Typeface direction: modern geometric sans-serif, bold/semi-bold.
Suggested production fonts: Inter / Manrope / Plus Jakarta Sans.

TAGLINE
IL TUO MUTUO, SEMPLICE
Uppercase, generous tracking, Navy or muted slate.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
1. Minimal first: avoid unnecessary symbols and visual clutter.
2. Software, not real estate agency: the identity should feel fintech/SaaS.
3. Flow is the key visual idea: process, continuity, speed, control.
4. Use the symbol alone for app icon/favicon.
5. Never add a house, shield, checkmark or bank icon to the primary mark.
6. Preserve generous clear space around the logo.
